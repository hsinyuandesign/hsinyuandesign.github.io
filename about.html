Who am I?

I'm a sophomore at UCSD, studying Cognitive Science (Design + Interaction). Before that, I was an artist, a developer, and an entrepreneur. Whilst these roles have taught me many things, there is one central theme that stays: the ability to think for others. That’s why I’m drawn to design – I am eager to turn human understanding into meaningful experiences that resonate with people.

To me, design is about seeing the world through someone else’s eyes– understanding their emotions, challenges, and needs, then creating something for them. It’s not just about solving problems, but also building connections and making everyday moments a little bit more thoughtful. It can be as simple as a student briefly pausing to notice a poster on their way to class, noticing something that makes them smile or think a little deeper.
